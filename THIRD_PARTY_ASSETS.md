# Third-party visual assets

The current visual foundation uses real photography delivered from `images.unsplash.com`; it does not use generated AI artwork.

Source platform: Unsplash
License information: https://unsplash.com/license

The image registry is centralised in `public/js/site-config.js`. Before final production release, RC should perform one final asset review and either:

1. keep approved Unsplash assets with the appropriate usage records; or
2. replace them with RC-owned / licensed commercial photography and self-host optimised WebP/AVIF derivatives.

Current imagery categories include:

- professional software/consulting teams
- cyber security technology
- computer hardware / AI infrastructure
- enterprise data centre / cloud infrastructure
- analytics dashboards / data engineering
- office IT support
- risk / business analytics
- strategy collaboration
- renewable energy / sustainability
- university / education
- automotive
- finance
- media production
- careers and corporate office environments

No random Google Image assets are used.
